FAQ

Good questions, honest answers.

How does PawMatch verify breeders?
Every breeder passes a 38-point audit: facility visit, vet records, pedigree validation, parent welfare, and ethical breeding practices. We re-audit annually.
Is the AI matching free?
Yes — AI Pet Matching is completely free. You only pay the breeder directly once you decide to bring a pet home.
What if my pet isn't the right fit?
Every adoption includes 30-day behavioural support from our trainers. In the rare case it isn't working, our breeder partners offer a re-home guarantee.
Do you deliver outside Bangkok?
We coordinate vetted pet transport to all 77 provinces in Thailand, with climate-controlled vehicles and a handler in the cabin.
